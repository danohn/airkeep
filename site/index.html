How it works

One relay keeps collecting readings and checking alert rules even when your apps are closed.

  1. Connect your printer

    The relay reads humidity and temperature from one P2S over your local network. Humidity describes the air around the filament.

  2. Run the relay

    Run Airkeep on an always-on host. It keeps seven days of history and serves your readings to signed-in clients over HTTPS.

  3. Set a humidity alert

    Choose a humidity threshold and how long it must be exceeded. Airkeep records when a rule triggers and when humidity recovers.
